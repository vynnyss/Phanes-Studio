### Prompt final da edição

Use case: style-transfer. Asset type: replacement Windows application icon for Phanes Studio. Input image 1 is the edit target. Redesign this ornate crystal-egg icon into an extremely minimal flat graphic emblem. Preserve only the core concept of a pointed cosmic seed and the gold/violet identity. Reduce it to a compact, symmetrical silhouette made of just three or four large solid geometric shapes: deep violet outer seed facets and one small warm gold diamond at the center suggesting the spark of creation. Smooth clean outlines, strong negative space, balanced square composition with 15 percent margins. Think a professional modern creative-software logo that reads instantly at 16 and 32 pixels. Completely flat colors, sharp vector-like edges. Remove all realistic rendering, textures, reflections, translucent facets, gradient shading, bloom, halos, glow, tiny lines, intricate details, and the round light orb. No text, letters, watermark, background panel, mockup or additional symbols. Keep a truly transparent background outside the mark. Produce one icon only.

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: Windows desktop application icon for Phanes Studio, a local AI-assisted 3D modeling application. Primary request: create one polished, distinctive icon inspired by Phanes, primordial creation and light emerging into geometric form. Subject: a luminous faceted cosmic egg or upright crystalline seed, with a compact radiant core and simple angular facets suggesting a 3D mesh being born. Composition: one centered emblem, square canvas, generous safe margins, bold silhouette that remains recognizable at 32 pixels. Style: restrained sculptural 3D emblem with crisp graphic edges and a few large facets, elegant professional creative software identity. Warm golden light with cool deep violet accents; no intricate ornament. Transparent background outside the emblem. Constraints: no text, letters, captions, watermark, mockup, desktop screenshot, extra symbols or multiple variations; avoid tiny details and sprawling rays.
